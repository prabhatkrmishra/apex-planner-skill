# Impact Analysis

Trace direct and transitive impact across source modules, callers, public/internal APIs, events, databases, caches, queues, scheduled jobs, auth boundaries, configuration/secrets, CI/CD, infrastructure, observability, tests, docs, external consumers and generated artifacts.

Ask: what calls this, what it calls, what data it changes, what assumptions consumers make, whether versions can coexist, what partial rollout does, whether retries/replays are safe, and whether rollback is physically reversible or requires compensation.
