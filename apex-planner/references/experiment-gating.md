# Experiment / Spike Gating

Use a spike/POC when a decision has high uncertainty and a wrong choice would be expensive to unwind.

A spike must define:
- hypothesis;
- smallest experiment;
- success/failure criteria;
- time/complexity budget;
- representative workload/data;
- evidence to collect;
- decision consequence.

Do not let a spike become unbounded implementation. Stop when the decision uncertainty is sufficiently reduced.
