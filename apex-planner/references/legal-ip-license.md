# Legal, IP and Licensing

Consider when relevant:
- third-party dependency licenses and transitive obligations;
- source-code/data/model licensing;
- generated-code ownership and provenance;
- trademark/brand constraints;
- open-source policy;
- data processing/consent obligations;
- export/control or jurisdiction constraints;
- contractual API/provider restrictions;
- attribution/notice requirements.

Do not provide legal conclusions from weak evidence. Identify the rule, jurisdiction, authoritative source and required human/legal gate when material.
