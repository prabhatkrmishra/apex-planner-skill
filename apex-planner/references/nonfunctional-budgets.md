# Non-Functional Budgets

Turn vague qualities into observable budgets when practical:

- latency: p50/p95/p99 or operation-specific target;
- reliability: availability/error budget;
- throughput/capacity: requests, jobs, records, concurrent users;
- resource: CPU, memory, storage, bandwidth;
- cost: per request/job/user or monthly ceiling;
- migration: maximum duration and acceptable read/write impact;
- security: explicitly acceptable residual risk and prohibited classes;
- operational load: alerts, on-call actions, manual steps.

Never invent precise numbers. Mark proposed budgets as proposals and identify how to baseline them.
