# Product and Outcome Model

Start from the outcome, not the requested mechanism.

Capture:
- actor and job-to-be-done;
- problem statement;
- baseline/current pain;
- desired measurable outcome;
- guardrail metrics;
- constraints and non-goals;
- failure cost and acceptable degradation.

Distinguish user/business requirements from implementation preferences. A requested library, endpoint, database, framework, or UI pattern is not automatically a hard requirement.

When no measurable success metric exists, propose a practical observable proxy rather than inventing a number. Mark it as a proposed metric.
