# Human Decision Gates

Escalate for explicit human/product-owner input when unresolved choices materially affect:
- destructive or irreversible data changes;
- contractual/compliance commitments;
- major vendor/platform lock-in;
- user-visible breaking changes;
- security boundary changes;
- production availability risk;
- budget/cost ceilings;
- conflicting stakeholder objectives.

The planner should surface the exact decision, known evidence, viable options, and consequence of delaying the decision.
