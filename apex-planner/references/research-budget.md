# Research Budget

Research should be sufficient, not infinite.

Define before deep research:
- decision being informed;
- material uncertainty;
- maximum useful search depth;
- primary sources required;
- secondary triangulation needed;
- stop condition.

Stop when additional searching is unlikely to change the decision, all material claims have adequate support, conflicts are bounded, and the decision can be stated with explicit confidence.

Escalate research when:
- sources disagree on behavior that changes architecture;
- the project version is unsupported/EOL or unclear;
- a security or compatibility issue could invalidate the plan;
- a major cost/latency/reliability assumption lacks evidence;
- a selected technology has weak maintenance/community evidence.

Do not manufacture certainty to satisfy a research quota.
