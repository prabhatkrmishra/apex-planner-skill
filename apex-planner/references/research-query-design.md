# Research Query Design

Form research in layers rather than issuing a single broad search:

1. Exact stack/version + official documentation.
2. Exact stack/version + release notes/migration/deprecation.
3. Exact stack/version + anti-pattern/pitfall/performance/security.
4. Architecture/problem term + authoritative engineering guidance.
5. Candidate alternatives + compatibility/lifecycle/security evidence.
6. Confirm any material claim with a primary source or a reproducible local check.

Prefer narrow queries that can falsify the planned approach. Search for known failure modes, limits, incompatibilities, and rollback constraints—not only confirming evidence.
